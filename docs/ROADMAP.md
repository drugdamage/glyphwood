# After Fragments & Expeditions

Version 0.14.0 implements biome-specific shops, targeted blueprint fragments,
four armour sidegrades, and chosen, shorter expeditions after the first descent.
It preserves existing gear and Normal combat values. The README animation is
an illustrated overview; a new gameplay capture waits for the next play session.

## Next playtest

The local game has deliberately not been launched for this update. Check these
in the next session before making further balance changes:

1. Continue the existing Normal world: the axe, iron set, reforges, skill tree
   and depth should be intact. Open **Pause → Blueprints** to see owned recipes
   already completed.
2. Pick an incomplete local blueprint. Open ordinary, arena and hidden chests;
   progress should be 1, 1 and 2 fragments respectively. After three fragments,
   buy the item at that biome's merchant. Save/continue and verify the same
   chest cannot award progress again.
3. Compare Cinder Veil, Marsh Wrap, Choir Hood and Rime Mail against the upgraded
   iron set. Check whether their utility is worth the protection tradeoff.
4. After the final guardian, use the exit and choose each expedition condition
   over successive floors. Verify route descriptions with keyboard, controller
   and mouse, and menus at 960×600, full screen and 130% interface scale.
5. Measure time per blueprint and per expedition. Aim for useful discoveries
   during one biome visit and substantially shorter postgame journeys. Tune
   fragments, prices or arena density only after observing actual play.
6. Record real gameplay of the journal, a completed recipe and an expedition
   transition, and replace/supplement the illustrated README overview.

## Further content — not implemented in 0.14.0

- **More expedition objectives:** rescue a trapped merchant, hunt a marked
  elite, recover a relic, or defend a moving lantern. Each needs map-generation
  guarantees, readable objectives and rewards that do not force one build.
- **New guardian variants:** new telegraphs, moves and arena layouts. Current
  expeditions remix existing enemies and use existing bosses; new boss designs
  remain a separate content pass.
- **More environmental combinations:** hand-made crossover rooms and unique
  landmark art. Current expeditions mix palettes and enemy pools, while keeping
  the selected biome's terrain rules and scenery family.
- **Long-term merchant progression:** rotating commissions or cosmetic mastery
  rewards once collections are complete. Current infinite shard sinks remain
  Vigor and Whetstone; fragments are a finite collection, not infinite gear tiers.
- **Armour investment transfer:** consider a paid partial reforge transfer if
  playtesting shows that sunk upgrade costs still discourage trying alternatives.
  No automatic transfer or old-equipment nerf is included now.

The regression suite covers progression, transactions, migration and seeded
maps. It does not establish live combat balance, full UI layout quality or
controller usability; those require the playtest above.
